Project One
Add Your Project Title

UX ResearchUser Testing FigmaMobile
Problem Statement
What problem are we solving?
Used-car buyers and sellers struggle with a lack of trust, transparency, and verification in existing resale platforms, where broker manipulation, fake listings, hidden commissions, and incomplete vehicle histories create uncertainty during transactions. First-time sellers fear pricing their vehicles incorrectly and dealing with legal or documentation issues, while buyers worry about tampered records, accident history, and non-genuine sellers. Busy professionals also face friction managing inspections, servicing, and resale processes across multiple disconnected systems. These challenges create hesitation, reduce confidence, and make the used-car resale experience stressful and inefficient for both buyers and sellers.
Solution
How did we solve it?
The solution is a verified used-car resale platform designed to build trust and reduce friction for both buyers and sellers. The app validates critical vehicle details such as RC, insurance, ownership records, and service history before listings are added to the catalogue, ensuring only genuine vehicles are displayed. Features like AI-assisted valuation, authenticity badges, transparent inspection reports, and simplified legal workflows help users make confident decisions while reducing dependency on brokers and unreliable marketplaces. The platform creates a more transparent, efficient, and trustworthy resale experience through verification-first design and centralized vehicle management.
