Problem Statement
What problem are we solving?
Users in the second-hand car market struggle with trust, transparency, and verification while buying or selling vehicles. Sellers fear low pricing and fraud, while buyers worry about fake documents, hidden damage, and unreliable listings, making genuine transactions difficult.
Solution
How did we solve it?
A verified car reselling platform that authenticates seller and vehicle details through backend checks like RC, insurance, and inspection verification before listings go live. Features like genuine vehicle badging, transparent valuation reports, service booking, and simplified legal workflows reduce trust issues and help users make faster, safer buying and selling decisions.

UX ResearchUsability Testing FigmaWeb App
Problem Statement
What problem are we solving?
Patients,Doctors and nurses in hospital struggle with fragmented workflows where appointments,reports,billing,monitoring and patient coordination are spread across multiple systems and departments.This creates delay communication gap,increased workload & difficulty accessing critical information during treatment and care management
Solution
How did we solve it?
A role based health care managment platforms with separate interfaces for patients,doctors and nurses designed to simplify hospitals workflows and improve coordination.Centralized appointments,reports,billing patient queus,OT scheduling,task tracking and vital monitoring reduce manual dependency,improve access to critical information and create a faster,more orgaised care experience for both patients and medical staff.
Empathy Map
👁
Says
  • (Doctor) Need patient reports and emergency updates quickly during rounds.
  • (Nurse) Managing multiple patients manually makes it easy to miss important tasks.
  • (Patient) Booking appointments should be simple even if I don’t know medical terms.
🧠
Thinks
  • (Patient) Hospitals feel confusing and stressful to navigate.
  • (Nurse) I need a more organized system to track investigations and vitals.
  • (Doctor) Depending too much on assistants slows down workflows during busy hours.
🎬
Does

❤️
Feels

  • (Patient) Frustrated by complicated hospital procedures and long navigation flows.
